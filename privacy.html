In short

This is a plain information website. It sets no cookies, loads no external fonts, maps or scripts, and uses no analytics or tracking. There is no contact form and no sign-up. Beyond what is technically unavoidable, no data is collected.

Server log files

This site is served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, GitHub processes technically necessary access data, including your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested, the amount of data transferred, and details about your browser and operating system.

This processing is required to deliver the site at all and to keep it secure and stable. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in the secure and reliable operation of the website. We have no access to these logs and do not evaluate them.

As GitHub is based in the United States, data may be transferred to a third country. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the European Commission's standard contractual clauses apply in addition. For details, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contacting us by email

If you write to us, we process your details solely to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where the enquiry concerns entering into or performing a contract, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR otherwise. We delete messages once they are no longer needed and no statutory retention periods apply.

Your rights
